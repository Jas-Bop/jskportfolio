# Support the Mission

SRFSC is entirely volunteer-run. Donations support firebreak work, hazard tree removal, community education, and preparedness efforts across Scripps Ranch.

## Why donate

Your support helps:

- maintain defensible space
- reduce wildfire fuel near canyon edges
- educate homeowners and families
- improve neighborhood readiness
