# Volunteer with SRFSC

We need neighbors who are ready to help protect the canyon and prepare the community.

## Ways to help

### Join a clearing day
Work alongside neighbors on canyon trails and firebreaks to reduce fuel and improve defensible space.

### Host a block meeting
Support residents by sharing fire-safety guidance and practical preparedness tips.

### Join the board
Help plan long-term strategy, outreach, and community safety work.

## Why it matters

Fire safety is a year-round effort. Small actions, done consistently, create safer homes and a more resilient neighborhood.
