# About SRFSC

The Scripps Ranch Fire Safe Council is a volunteer-led neighborhood organization focused on wildfire prevention and community preparation.

## Our purpose

We work to reduce wildfire risk by clearing fuel, supporting defensible space, and educating residents. We believe that protecting the canyon and the homes around it requires everyday community action.

## Our values

- neighbor-led action
- wildfire preparedness
- community education
- practical safety work

## Why it matters

Scripps Ranch sits near canyon terrain that has historically increased fire risk. Our work helps give firefighters a safer environment and residents more time to act during an emergency.
